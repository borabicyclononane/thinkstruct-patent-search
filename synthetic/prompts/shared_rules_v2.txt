Formatting rules for every claim you write:
- Write a complete independent patent claim in proper form: a preamble naming the
  invention (e.g. "A wheel assembly" or "A method of ..."), a transitional phrase such
  as "comprising:", then the elements separated by semicolons, with "and" before the
  last element, ending in a single period.
- No claim number, no reference numerals (e.g. "unit 100"), no figure references.
- Use precise technical language.
- Do not reuse the patent title's wording.
Return JSON only, no other text.
